# EcoLens: 30-second video (fallback to the slides)

Use this only if the team prefers a video to the seven slides. Rules (docs/SUBMISSION.md): **30
seconds at most**, public link with no login, English (or another language with English
subtitles), no images of people under 18. No NASA logo or mission identifier on screen. If any
frame or voice is generated by AI, it needs a visible watermark or a declaration; this plan uses
only screen recordings of the app and a human voice, so none is needed.

## How to record

- Screen recording of the live app at 1440 × 900 (QuickTime: File → New Screen Recording),
  in a clean browser window with no bookmarks bar.
- Shots 3–6 come from the guided tour: open https://ecolens-earth.vercel.app/tour. It opens on
  the first solved case, **Delhi, sunlight reaching the ground**. Its beats last about 3, 7, 6.5,
  6 and 6.5 seconds, so the recording must be **cut and sped up** to fit; use the tour's
  forward button (›) to jump between beats.
- Add burned-in captions (the text in the "Caption" column) so the video works without sound.
- Export as MP4, 1080p, and check the final length is **≤ 30.0 s** before uploading
  (YouTube "unlisted" or Google Drive "anyone with the link").
